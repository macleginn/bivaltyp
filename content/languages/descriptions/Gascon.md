## Grammar notes

### Basic clause structure and the transitive construction

Gascon displays the nominative-accusative alignment. Nominal core arguments (A, S and O) remain unflagged, so that the distinction between subject and direct object is only indicated by word order (SVO). By contrast, pronominal object clitics are used before the verb. They come in a fixed order and display three distinct cases. Nominal arguments other than A, S and O are flagged by prepositions. Subjects (A and S) are indexed on the verbs. In some contexts, participles in analytic constructions also signal the number and gender of the direct object. Gascon is a pro-drop language.

### Case system

Gascon has no morphological case on nouns. Personal pronouns have three case-marked clitic forms: nominative, accusative, and dative. The dative form of a pronoun usually corresponds to the combination of the preposition *a* with the noun. Many varieties of the language have a DOM system, where some human and definite direct objects are marked with the same preposition *a*.
