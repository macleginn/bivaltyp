## Grammar notes

### Basic clause structure and the transitive construction

The neutral word order in a simple affirmative clause is S(O)V. The preverbal position is reserved for focus. Lezgian has no gender category. Nouns, pronouns, and nominalized dependents are inflected for case and number. Verbal inflection expresses tense–aspect–mood and polarity distinctions. Both nominal and verbal morphology are agglutinative and predominantly suffixing. However, prefixes occur in negation and verbal derivation, the latter involving non-productive locative preverbs and reversive preverbs. Non-verbal predication is expressed by means of copulas. Grammatical relations are marked by case and postpositions. The coding properties indicate ergative alignment: S = P ≠ A.

There are two morphological classes of synthetic verbs: ‘weak’ and ‘strong’ verbs. ‘Weak’ verbs end in a consonant and have a single stem for all verbal forms, as illustrated in (1) below. ‘Strong’ verbs contain a thematic vowel attached to the verbal base before any other ending. Depending on this vowel, these verbs may have one, two, or three stems, as illustrated in (2). Some verbs have suppletive stems, as shown in (3).

### Case system

Yargun Lezgian has 15 cases, including 4 grammatical cases and 11 spatial cases. The absolutive is unmarked. The ergative is formed from the absolutive by means of the following suffixes: *-di*, *-a*, *-e*, *-i*, *-u*, *-ü*, *-adi*, *-edi*, *-ra*, *-re*, *-uni*, *-üni*, *-ini*, *-čči*, *-č’i*, *-ani*, *-eni*, *-cci*, *-c’i*, *-c’u*, *-dji*. The ergative serves as the oblique stem for all other cases. The spatial case system consists of two layers: 4 localizations (IN ‘inside’, AD ‘at, near’, SUB/POST ‘behind, under’, SUP ‘on’) and 3 directionals (the unmarked locative, directive, and elative). In standard Lezgian, the postlocative (‘behind’) and sublocative (‘under’) are formed by suffixing the morphemes *-qh* and *-k*, respectively, to the oblique stem. In Yargun Lezgian, these forms have undergone syncretism in favour of *-k*.
